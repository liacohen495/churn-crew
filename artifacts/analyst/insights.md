# Business Insights — Telco Customer Churn

**27% of 7,043 customers churned**, taking
**$139,131 in monthly revenue** with them.

## Key findings
1. **Contract type is the strongest signal.** Month-to-month: 43% churn,
   two-year contracts: 3%.
2. **New customers leave most.** First-year customers churn at 47%;
   customers past 4 years at 10%.
3. **Fiber optic customers churn more** (42%) than DSL
   (19%).
4. **Electronic-check payers churn at 45%**, the highest of any payment method.
5. **Support matters.** No tech support: 42% churn vs 15% with it.

## Recommendations
- Offer month-to-month customers an incentive to move to a 1-year contract.
- Build an onboarding programme for the first 12 months.
- Bundle tech support / online security for fiber customers.
- Encourage automatic payment methods.
