## Key findings

1. **Short-Tenure Risk:** New customers in their first year (0–12 months) experience the highest churn rate at 47.44%, compared to 28.71% for 13–24 months, 20.39% for 25–48 months, and 9.51% for 49–72 months.
2. **Payment Method Disparity:** Customers paying via Electronic check churn at 45.29%, compared to lower churn among automatic Bank transfer (16.71%), Credit card (15.24%), and Mailed check (19.11%) users.
3. **Contract Flexibility Impact:** Month-to-month contract subscribers exhibit a 42.71% churn rate, sharply contrasting with One year (11.27%) and Two year (2.83%) contract holders.
4. **Internet Service Line Vulnerability:** Fiber optic internet subscribers churn at a rate of 41.89%, compared to DSL users at 18.96% and non-internet users at 7.40%.
5. **Add-On Service Protection:** Customers lacking Online Security churn at 41.77% (vs. 14.61% with service) and those without Tech Support churn at 41.64% (vs. 15.17% with service).

## Recommendations

1. **Contract Conversion Incentives:** Launch targeted onboarding campaigns for 0–12 month customers to convert Month-to-month contracts (42.71% churn) into One year (11.27% churn) or Two year (2.83% churn) commitments.
2. **Automatic Payment Migration:** Offer billing discounts to migrate customers paying via Electronic check (45.29% churn) to automated options like Credit card (15.24% churn) or Bank transfer (16.71% churn).
3. **Fiber Optic Retention & Service Review:** Evaluate pricing and service delivery for Fiber optic subscribers (41.89% churn) to address satisfaction gaps relative to DSL users (18.96% churn).
4. **Protective Service Bundling:** Automatically package or discount Online Security and Tech Support for internet subscribers to reduce churn from over 41% (41.77% and 41.64%) toward protected rates of 14.61% and 15.17%.
